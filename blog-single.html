Scaling Vector Databases for Billion-Scale Datasets

Sarah Chen

March 15, 2025 · 8 min read

As we transitioned from a small-scale prototype to a production-grade system handling millions of queries per day, we hit a bottleneck: our vector search latency was spiking.

The Challenge of High Dimensionality

Traditional databases leverage B-trees for efficient indexing. However, embeddings exist in high-dimensional space (often 1536 dimensions for OpenAI models), rendering traditional indexing methods ineffective due to the curse of dimensionality.

Implementing HNSW

Hierarchical Navigable Small World (HNSW) graphs became our solution. By creating a multi-layered graph structure, we reduced search complexity from O(N) to O(log N).

"Premature optimization is the root of all evil, but database latency is the killer of user experience."

We saw a 40x improvement in query speeds after migrating our core indexing engine.
